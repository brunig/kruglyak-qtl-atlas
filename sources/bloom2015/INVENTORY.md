## Not in the bundle

- No trait doses: the 20 trait names carry no concentration, and the article says phenotyping was 'as described previously' (Bloom et al. 2013). See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
- No genome-wide LOD profiles for the reported QTL (the genotype and phenotype matrices in Supplementary Data 5 would allow recomputing them; the atlas does not).
- No rule for matching full-scan and marginal-scan interactions (the text's '172 detected by both' cannot be reproduced exactly).
- No statement of the scale of lm_coefficient beyond the -1/1 genotype coding of Supplementary Data 5.
