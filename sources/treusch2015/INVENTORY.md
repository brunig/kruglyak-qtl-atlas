## Not in the bundle

- No genome-wide LOD or allele-frequency traces: the paper deposits VCFs, mapping data and R scripts on Dryad (doi:10.5061/dryad.j20f0), which is not reachable from the build environment.
- No per-cross LOD or interval for grouped QTL: S4 gives one combined LOD, max-LOD position and interval per group, and the list of crosses.
- No effect sizes: X-QTL report allele-frequency LOD; direction is given only as strains with beneficial or deleterious alleles (S7).
- No segregant counts per pool (500,000 sorted cells per mating type; Methods).
