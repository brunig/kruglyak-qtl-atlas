What this shows. The atlas shows what the papers reported, row for row, with every original column kept and a provenance key (file, sheet, row) on each QTL. Candidate genes carry the paper's evidence tier (previously identified, local FDR 5%, local FDR 20%) and are not claimed as causal beyond it. Nothing here is re-mapped or re-estimated.

Bloom et al. 2019. Within-cross QTL are drawn as their 1.5-LOD intervals; joint-model QTL as their lead variant, because the paper reports no interval for them. Effects are in standard deviations of colony radius per standard deviation of genotype; positive joint effects mean the non-reference allele makes larger colonies. Which parent is genotype 1 in each cross is inferred (the alphabetically first strain label, which reproduces the candidate-gene table's effect labels in all 16 crosses); see open questions.

Coordinates. S288C reference, R64 (sacCer3). Genes. SGD annotation R64-5-1, for display. Heatmap. QTL per 50 kb bin, counting each interval in every bin it touches.
